# New subjects and topics: proposal for review

Date: 2026-10-10. Status: **draft for the owner's approval**. Nothing has changed on the site yet.

## What was decided (owner, 2026-10-10)

- Clean up topics as well as subjects.
- Quantitative Aptitude and Logical Reasoning become their own subject, **Reasoning & Aptitude**.
- The three History subjects (Ancient, Medieval, Modern) stay separate.
- Tennis, Cricket, Football and other sports become one **Sports** topic under Current Affairs.
- The new names are applied at the source data, so the website, the search server and the search filter all use the same names.

## The result in numbers

- **13 subjects become 11.** International Relations (5 questions), Governance (2) and Sports (1) fold into proper subjects, and Reasoning & Aptitude (253) is added.
- **203 topics become 116.** No topic has fewer than 5 questions any more. Today 82 do.
- **All 3,981 questions are placed.** None are dropped or duplicated.
- **Students' marks and lists are not affected.** They are stored by year and question number, never by subject.

## How the merges were chosen

- **Near-duplicates go into the main topic.** For example, *Pollution (3)* goes into *Pollution & Waste Management*, and *Fundamental Duties (4)* joins *Fundamental Rights* as *Fundamental Rights & Duties*.
- **A few questions move subject** where their label was clearly wrong:
  - The English and Portuguese factory questions go to Modern History's *Advent of Europeans*.
  - A Polity "awards" question goes to Current Affairs.
  - A Science "business functions" puzzle goes to Reasoning & Aptitude.
- **A few topics are renamed** to say what they hold, e.g. *Industry, Services & Infrastructure*, *Bhakti & Sufi Movements*, *Regional Kingdoms & the Marathas*.
- **Questions from catch-all topics** ("Ancient India", "World Geography", "Medieval India", "Modern India") were read and placed one by one. They are listed at the end.

Anything you'd name or group differently, tell me and I'll change it before we build.
